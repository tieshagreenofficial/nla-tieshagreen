NLA is not three days of tactics and templates. We will absolutely connect what we're doing to visibility, sales, opportunity, income and business growth. But we're starting with the person who has to execute all those strategies. Because even the best strategy still has to be carried by you.

No. NLA is an educational and coaching experience focused on identity, authority, self-perception and behavior in the context of your goals, work and leadership. It is not therapy, mental-health treatment or a substitute for care from a qualified mental-health professional.

Quite the opposite. One of the central ideas we're challenging is whether you've accidentally organized your growth around finding the next thing that's wrong with you. NLA asks a different question: given what you now know about who you are, how does this person move?
