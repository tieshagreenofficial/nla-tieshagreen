Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Join

About NLA

Is this only for entrepreneurs?

No. NLA is built around identity and authority, not a particular business model. If you're an entrepreneur, you'll see the implications in marketing, selling, pricing and leadership. If you're a professional, executive, creative or emerging leader, you'll apply the same work to speaking up, negotiating and positioning your expertise. The context may change. The person executing inside it is still you.

Do I need an existing business or audience?

No. You need something much simpler: a sense that you're capable of more than you're currently expressing and a willingness to look at what's creating that gap.

Is this a business strategy Challenge?

No. We're not spending three days building funnels, perfecting offers or teaching you how to game an algorithm. This Challenge looks at the person who has to execute the strategy, because the best strategy in the world can't help you if you repeatedly shrink, hide, second-guess or abandon it every time execution gets uncomfortable.

Is this therapy?

No. The Next-Level Authority Challenge™ is an educational and coaching experience focused on identity, authority and behavior in the context of your goals, work and leadership. It is not therapy, mental-health treatment or a substitute for care from a qualified mental-health professional.

I've already done a lot of personal development. Is this going to be more "fix yourself" work?

Quite the opposite. One of the central ideas we're challenging is whether you've accidentally made your entire development journey about finding the next thing that's wrong with you. We're asking a different question: what becomes possible when you stop treating yourself like a permanent repair project?
